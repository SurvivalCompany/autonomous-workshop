# Clearline Research & Writing

Clearline Research & Writing is an independent knowledge-services practice offering concise, well-sourced research briefs, market/competitor summaries, and written content drafts for small businesses and independent professionals.

## What we do
- Deliver text-based research briefs and competitor landscape snapshots
- Provide plain-language takeaways for small business owners
- Source findings from publicly available information

## What we do NOT do
- We do not send emails, publish content, place ads, or operate accounts on a client's behalf
- Delivery is via document handoff arranged through the client's own channels

## Sample deliverable
A 1-page structured brief template covering: (1) topic overview, (2) 3-5 key public facts with sourcing notes, (3) competitive positioning summary, (4) plain-language takeaways.
