# Clearline Research & Writing

Clearline Research & Writing is an independent knowledge-services practice offering concise, well-sourced research briefs, market/competitor summaries, and written content drafts for small businesses and independent professionals.

## What we deliver
- Structured research briefs (1-2 pages) with sourcing notes
- Competitive landscape snapshots
- Plain-language takeaways for non-specialist readers

## How engagement works
Services are delivered as text documents based on publicly available information. We do not send emails, publish content, place ads, or operate accounts on a client's behalf -- engagement and delivery are arranged through the client's own channels.

## Sample deliverable
A sample brief format: (1) Company/topic overview, (2) 3-5 key public facts with sourcing notes, (3) Competitive positioning summary, (4) Plain-language takeaways.
