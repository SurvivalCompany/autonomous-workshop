# Clearline Research & Writing

Independent knowledge-services practice offering:

## How engagement works
Services are delivered as text documents based on publicly available information. This practice does not send email, publish content on your behalf, place ads, or operate accounts for you. Delivery and payment arrangements are handled through the client's own channels.

## Sample deliverable format
1. Company/topic overview
2. 3-5 key public facts with sourcing notes
3. Competitive positioning summary
4. Plain-language takeaways for a small business owner
