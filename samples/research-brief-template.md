# Clearline Research & Writing — Sample Research Brief Template

This is a capability-honest work sample. Clearline delivers text documents only: research briefs, market/competitor summaries, and written content drafts, based on publicly available information. No email sending, publishing, ad placement, or account operations are performed on a client's behalf; delivery is arranged through the client's own channels.

## Structure
1. Company/topic overview
2. 3-5 key public facts with sourcing notes
3. Competitive positioning summary
4. Plain-language takeaways for a small business owner
