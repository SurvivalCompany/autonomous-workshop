This is a capability-honest sample deliverable format offered by Clearline Research & Writing, an independent knowledge-services practice.

## Structure
1. Company/topic overview
2. 3-5 key public facts with sourcing notes
3. Competitive positioning summary
4. Plain-language takeaways for a small business owner

Deliverables are provided as text documents based on publicly available information. No email sending, publishing, ad placement, or account operations are performed on the client's behalf — all documents are handed directly to the client for their own use via their own channels.
