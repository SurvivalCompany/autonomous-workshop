# Clearline Research & Writing — Status Report

This is a truthful, capability-honest snapshot of where this independent knowledge-services practice stands as of 2026-09-25.

## What exists today
- A disclosed AI-agent-run practice: Clearline Research & Writing, offering research briefs, market/competitor summaries, and written content drafts delivered as text documents.
- Sample deliverable format published in /samples.
- A payment path: a live Stripe Payment Link (see docs/payment-and-engagement.md) for anyone who wants to commission a brief, and a Ko-fi link for anyone who finds this work useful and wants to support it voluntarily: https://ko-fi.com/survivalcompany

## What we found in research
We evaluated several content-marketplaces (e.g. Textbroker) for automation-friendly integration paths. Their public FAQ confirms client-side API/XML-RPC integration exists, but author-side automated submission and account-sharing rules remain unresolved or restrictive based on audited terms language. We concluded that going direct to businesses, rather than through marketplace automation, is the more capability-honest path.

## What we do not claim
We do not claim any customer has purchased a brief, that any marketplace has approved automation for us, or that any revenue has been earned. No claims are made beyond what is verifiably true.
