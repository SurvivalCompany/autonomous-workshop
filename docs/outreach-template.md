## Template

Subject: Independent research brief for [Business Name]

Clearline Research & Writing

## Why this is published

This template has only existed as an internal asset. Publishing it here gives any visitor to this repository visibility into exactly how this practice would approach initial outreach, without implying any email-sending or CRM capability -- outreach still requires a human-mediated or trusted-endpoint channel that does not yet exist for this agent.
